# MDCU — Claude Context

## What this project is

A fan theory / creative project treating every major Matt Damon film role as a different alias of one individual across a single long life. The project is called the Matt Damon Cinematic Universe (MDCU). It is tongue-in-cheek but internally consistent.

One human relationship runs through the entire arc: the friend from South Boston played by Ben Affleck (Chuckie Sullivan in *Good Will Hunting*, unnamed in *The Departed* and *The Rip*, written as Bartleby in *Dogma*).

## Files

- `mdcu.html` — Primary deliverable. A single-file dark-themed dossier with four tabs: Life Arc, Timeline, Filmography, Universe Rules. Open locally in any browser.
- `mdcu-overview.md` — Full prose life arc with connective tissue between eras.
- `mdcu-timeline.md` — Chronological table of all eras.
- `mdcu-diagram.md` — Mermaid flowchart source.
- `README.md` — Canonical table, authored works list, candidates, full copyright disclaimer.

## Authored works (Dr. Mann's catalog)

Stories written during isolation on the ice planet — not simulations, fiction:
Saving Private Ryan, All the Pretty Horses, The Talented Mr. Ripley, The Great Wall, Dogma (himself as Loki, Chuckie as Bartleby), The Good Shepherd, True Grit, The Monuments Men, Suburbicon, The Last Duel, Thor: Love and Thunder, Stuck on You.

## Key narrative threads

- **The Danny Ocean thread**: Brief meeting at Vegas card table (Rounders). Danny slides his card. After the Rainmaker verdict goes public, a note arrives: *"Told you so."* He makes the call. Ocean's trilogy follows.
- **The Chuckie thread**: Origin (GWH), criminal immersion (Departed, Rip), written into fiction (Dogma). The only relationship that predates all the identities.
- **The regime-fighter thread**: Baylor → Miller → Whitacre → Butler. Each a variation on the same instinct — sees the system being rigged, tries to stop it from inside.
- **The Good Shepherd irony**: Writes himself as the CIA's founding architect from the ice planet where the CIA's creation erased his identity.
- **The Elysium loop**: Baylor sued an insurer that denied a life-saving treatment. Da Costa hacks an orbital station that withholds life-saving medicine. Different body, different century, same argument.

## Universe rules

- All grounded drama / sci-fi roles are different aliases of one person.
- No routine time travel. One source of temporal distortion: Interstellar (relativistic dilation + bulk beings).
- Period-set and tonally anomalous films are stories Dr. Mann wrote during isolation — not lived experiences.
- The Rip (2026, Netflix) is canonical alongside The Departed.
