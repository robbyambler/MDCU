# MDCU Personal Chronology

Approximate dates are from an Earth frame of reference. The character's subjective experience stretches across these eras due to long-duration missions and relativistic effects in *Interstellar*.

| Personal era | Approx. era (Earth) | Film / alias | MDCU interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Early legal crusader | Mid-1990s | *The Rainmaker* – Rudy Baylor | Young Memphis lawyer who sues a corrupt insurer over a denied bone marrow transplant, witnessing how profit-driven systems can calmly let people die. |
| Wounded prodigy in Boston | Late 1990s | *Good Will Hunting* – Will Hunting | Retreats to South Boston under a different identity, living as a janitor and self-taught math prodigy while hiding his earlier legal career and brushes with the FBI. |
| Military crucible | Early 2000s | *Green Zone* – Roy Miller | As a U.S. Army Chief Warrant Officer in Iraq, leads a unit hunting for WMDs and discovers that the intelligence justifying the war is fabricated. |
| Black-ops creation | Early–mid 2000s | *Bourne* series – "Jason Bourne" / David Webb | Recruited into a clandestine program; memories are stripped, past identities buried, and a new persona engineered as a deniable CIA asset. |
| Undercover rot | Mid-2000s | *The Departed* – Colin Sullivan | Serves as a long-term deep-cover Boston cop aligned with organized crime, balancing loyalties and further corroding his moral center. |
| Corporate fracture | Late 2000s | *The Informant!* – Mark Whitacre | Lives as an agribusiness executive and informant whose paranoia and manic behavior reflect the toll of juggling lies, cover stories, and fractured memory. |
| Global energy cover | Mid–late 2000s | *Syriana* – Bryan Woodman | Operates as a Geneva-based energy analyst and adviser to a Gulf prince, witnessing how oil politics, corporate mergers, and covert ops reshape entire regions. |
| Glimpse behind the curtain | Around 2010s | *The Adjustment Bureau* – David Norris | Reinvented as a rising New York politician, he encounters the Adjustment Bureau—agents who step through doors to enforce a hidden Plan for human history. |
| Planet-scale stakes | Early 2010s | *Contagion* – Mitch Emhoff | Survives a lethal global pandemic and appears mysteriously immune, reinforcing his status as a statistically improbable survivor. |
| Escape to space | 2030s | *The Martian* – Mark Watney | Becomes a NASA astronaut and botanist; stranded on Mars and presumed dead, he survives via engineering creativity and rigorous math. |
| Return, guilt, and recruitment | 2040s–2050s | Post-*Martian*, pre-*Interstellar* gap | Returns to a Blight-ravaged Earth, quietly teaches and consults, then volunteers for a one-way Lazarus mission as atonement for past roles in policy and exploration. |
| Exile by wormhole | Late 21st century | *Interstellar* – Dr. Mann | Under the alias Dr. Mann, leads an interstellar expedition through a wormhole and lands on an ice world, eventually falsifying data in hopes of rescue. |
| Relativistic drift | Concurrent with Endurance mission | *Interstellar* – Dr. Mann (continued) | Experiences significant time dilation near a black hole, so his subjective years of waiting correspond to much longer spans on Earth. |
| Far-future training / mythic loop | Far future (relative to origin) | *The Great Wall* – William Garin (simulation) | In a far-future context, his consciousness participates in a simulation or mythic reconstruction that casts him as a mercenary defending the Great Wall against monstrous invaders. |
| Class war endgame | 22nd century | *Elysium* – Max Da Costa | Reappears in a polluted, class-stratified world where elites live on Elysium; he hacks the system to universalize access to advanced medicine, completing his arc against rigged systems. |
